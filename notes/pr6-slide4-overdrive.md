## System from the kit
- Type: Helvetica Neue Bold tight (wordmark, big numbers); Regular/Medium large calm headlines; Menlo caps +0.16–0.3em at 8–11 px for labels/codes/actions. Free: TeX Gyre Heros / Nimbus Sans + DejaVu Sans Mono.
- Colour: Chalk F2F3EF, Mist CAD5DA, Slab ~B1BDC6, Steel 718B9A, Ink 171C1F, Orange E98546 (mark only). Unused deep register from slides 9/10: Deep 3F668F, Sky A5C7DB.
- Layout: wide margins, object off-centre, text top-left, action bottom-right, hairline rules with labels at both ends, edge indices, "+ ×" corner marks.
- Texture: grain on mist; frosted glass with bevel; brushed/embossed metal.
- Motion: slow and physical (blur to focus, drift, turn, step forward). No light-change effects (lu, 3 Nov).
